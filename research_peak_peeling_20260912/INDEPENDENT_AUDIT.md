# Independent peak-peeling finite audit — 2026-09-12

**Verdict:** independently computed W and W_flat match exactly at r=12 and r=20, including every contributing k stratum. The tail-selected shares are numerical diagnostics, not certified individual spectral coefficients or asymptotic bounds. No source or Git mutation was performed; this audit writes only this note and INDEPENDENT_RESULTS.json.

## Independent arithmetic route

The audit used scalar Python integers, Fraction, and no NumPy, FFT, probe imports, or copied binomial shell-budget function. For every odd h in [1,2^m), it executed m shortcut steps, counting oddness before each step, and accumulated the exact endpoint histogram P_k mod q. Tail indicators were separately generated by scalar t-step iteration from each residue.

For every contributing k, all q translated counts were computed directly:

    G_k(a) = sum_z P_k(z) T_(r-k)(z+a).
    V_k = sum_a G_k(a)^2/q - (sum_a G_k(a)/q)^2.

W is the exact sum of these rational variances. Omitting the odd dilation 3^k in the translation parameter is valid for this per-k variance: multiplication by 3^k permutes all residues mod q. This does not claim that the similarly reparameterized *sum across k* has the same total-count variance.

Independently, for every s the source and tail histograms were aggregated mod 2^s. Their squared half-differences R_source and R_tail were computed directly, without the combinatorial D formula. The flat baseline contribution is

    2^(s-1) R_source R_tail / q^2.

Summing gives W_flat. Thus the comparison simultaneously checks the probe's binomial D against the actual tail aggregation after multiplication by the actual source factor, and checks the exact variances against a route that does not use Ramanujan correlations.

## Integer overflow review

For this fixed panel range, m<=24. For x_0<2^m, the shortcut inequality x_(i+1)+1 <= (3/2)(x_i+1) gives x_i+1 <= 3^i 2^(m-i). The largest evaluated branch expression 3*x+1 is therefore bounded by 2*3^24=564859072962, far below signed int64 maximum 9223372036854775807. NumPy evaluates both np.where branches, so bounding this expression rather than only the selected next value is necessary.

Prefix histograms and their aggregated bins are nonnegative and bounded by the total population 2^23. The integer dot product P@T sums at most 2^23 because T is a zero/one indicator. Histogram indexing k*q+x%q is also bounded by (m+1)q. Source half-differences fit int64 before conversion. Correlation products and their sums in the probe are explicitly converted to Python int, and exact variances use Fraction. These checks establish safety for the frozen r=5..20 experiment; extending the range requires repeating the bounds.

## Selection and normalization review

- Selection is within each (k,s), not a global top-L selection across W. For s>=2, odd representatives 1<=a<2^(s-1) identify each conjugate pair once and the factor is 2. For s=1 the self-conjugate frequency is counted once. L larger than the available number of pairs selects all of them.
- Tail grouping and ordering use only the tail autocorrelation / tail FFT values. Exact equality classes are found by reducing the integer autocorrelation polynomial evaluated at zeta^a modulo Phi_(2^s)=X^(2^(s-1))+1. Equality of the resulting coefficient vectors is the correct exact equality test. Members of one exact tie class are ordered by increasing representative, independently of source energy.
- Different equality classes are ordered numerically by FFT tail magnitude, with a floating separation assertion. This assertion is a useful numerical separation check, not an interval or symbolic order certificate. The exact equality grouping fixes tie selection but does not by itself certify every remaining strict ranking.
- Joint-product ranking is correctly separated as a source-dependent diagnostic oracle. It is not the tail-only selection rule.
- Removed contributions are divided by the original W. The residual is divided by the original W_flat. No residual-baseline renormalization is performed.
- All 16 panels and all three L values passed 48 numerical sanity checks at tolerance 1e-12: shares in [0,1] up to roundoff, tail-selected share no larger than joint oracle, monotonic selected share in L, nonnegative residual up to roundoff, and residual/original-flat=(1-tail-share)*(W/W_flat).

Some fully peeled low-conductor panels print shares such as 1.0000000000000002 or tiny negative residuals of order 1e-16. INDEPENDENT_RESULTS.json records these raw roundoff excursions. They are not negative exact variances; a human-facing table should round them to 100% / zero while retaining raw diagnostics. No correction to the underlying integer calculation is needed.

## Interpretation and limits

At r=20, tail-only top-1,2,4 selection captures approximately 26.8%, 31.1%, 67.6% of W; corresponding residuals/original-W_flat are approximately 0.9382, 0.8828, 0.4158. The joint oracle captures substantially more, confirming that source-tail alignment matters in this finite panel. These are FFT diagnostics consistent with the exact layer totals, not certified pairwise shares. Agreement of an FFT sum with exact V does not certify all constituent terms or their ranking.

The exact finite residual enclosure in ANALYTIC_REVIEW.md requires a valid upper bound for each unselected multiplier (or certified ordering plus a certified next multiplier). This experiment does not turn its rounded FFT values into such an enclosure. Nor do the finite panels prove fixed-L control for growing conductors, source-energy bounds on the selected peaks, a W/D2 asymptotic exponent, or a Collatz orbit exclusion.
