## 4. Landscape placement (what each gives; BELOW / PARALLEL / POTENTIALLY BEYOND)

- [JUDGEMENT] Terras/Everett stopping-time density: I understand this to give that almost all positive integers have finite stopping time (some iterate drops below the start), proved by combining the exact parity-vector distribution with a coefficient-size argument and a density limit; the programme's assets are BELOW it, because the programme owns the finite ingredients (A1, binomial masses, range) but has not assembled the density limit, which is exactly the missing infinite step.
- [JUDGEMENT] Tao almost-all theorem: I understand this to give Collatz for almost all integers in a logarithmic-density sense via Syracuse random variables and L^p-type control; the programme is BELOW it by a large margin, and its known ≥1/2 Fourier-coefficient obstruction confirms the programme has no route to this level with current tools.
- [JUDGEMENT] 2-adic conjugacy picture: I understand this to give that the parity-vector map is a measure-preserving conjugacy of 2-adic integers to the one-sided shift (every 2-adic has a parity sequence and vice versa), which explains barrier B2 (compactness yields a 2-adic realiser, not an integer one); A2 is BELOW it as a finite fragment/lemma, and completing A2 to the full conjugacy would reproduce a known theorem rather than extend it.
- [JUDGEMENT] Steiner and Simons–de Weger cycle exclusions: I understand these to give exclusion of 1-cycles and 2-cycles (and large lower bounds on any cycle) via linear forms in logarithms (Baker theory) plus computation; the programme's cycle enumeration is BELOW them, because finite integrality filtering does not touch the infinite families that Baker theory excludes, and the brief correctly forbids re-deriving them.
- [JUDGEMENT] Applegate–Lagarias computational work: I understand this to give large-scale verification (all integers below a huge bound reach 1) plus tree/coefficient statistics with engineered sieves; the programme's exact tables are PARALLEL in spirit (exact finite enumeration) but BELOW in scale and engineering, so no competition on bounds.
- [JUDGEMENT] Krasikov–Lagarias density bounds: I understand this to give an explicit positive-exponent lower bound (x^0.84-type) on how many integers ≤ x reach 1, via difference inequalities on the convergent-set counting function; the programme is BELOW it, and asset A9 (caps/supports/masses/integrality insufficient, 91-interval witness) is positive evidence the current tools cannot reach it without new inequalities.

## 5. Best validation-reproduction candidate and feasibility

- [JUDGEMENT] Best candidate: reprove the Terras/Everett density-1 finite-stopping-time statement with the programme's machinery, because it consumes exactly what the programme owns (A1, binomial masses n_k, exact per-(m,k) B maxima) plus one standard probability tail bound, and it directly tests whether the signed (h,k,B) assignment data can drive an infinite conclusion.
- [PROOF] Proof skeleton the reproduction would follow: fix (m,k) with 3^k < 2^m, bound max B over that stratum exactly (finite table, cf. A6 stabilisation), note Hp^m(h) = (3^k·h + B)/2^m < h whenever h > maxB/(2^m − 3^k), so all large h in dropping strata have finite stopping time; then show via a standard binomial tail bound that the union of dropping strata has density → 1 as m → ∞, and iterate the dropping step to get finite stopping time density 1.
- [JUDGEMENT] Feasibility estimate: high and concrete — the per-(m,k) maxima are exact integer computations at modest m (start m ≈ 30–60, big integers only, no floats), the tail bound is textbook Chernoff/weak-law material with no new machinery, and the density-limit plus iteration argument is standard; I estimate roughly one to two weeks for a careful write-up with exact scripts, with the main risk being clean handling of the B-maxima uniformity and the density-limit bookkeeping, both routine rather than research-level.
- [JUDGEMENT] Second-best candidate: complete A2 to the full 2-adic conjugacy (parity map is a measure-preserving bijection of 2-adic integers); also feasible from A2 plus compactness, but less Collatz-specific and further from the cycle question, so strictly less valuable as validation.
- [JUDGEMENT] Explicit non-targets: Tao (needs L^p/Syracuse machinery the programme lacks), Krasikov–Lagarias (needs difference inequalities; A9 shows current ingredients insufficient), Steiner/Simons–de Weger (needs Baker theory; brief forbids re-derivation), large-bound verification à la Applegate–Lagarias (needs sieve engineering; feasible but low novelty per unit effort).
