# Audit of the proposed inverse-convolution bridge

2026-09-08. Exploratory mathematical result; no new Collatz exclusion. Source: Shmerkin, https://arxiv.org/html/1907.07121 , Section 2, especially the distinction between a large correlation value and failure of exponential L^q flattening. The theorem is not applied here.

## A missing implication is false for general histograms

Normalize actual prefix and demand counts to probability measures mu=P/n and nu=D/C (when C>0). Their normalized join is (mu * reflected(nu))(0). A large value at this ONE translation does not imply the convolution retains a large fraction of the input L^2 norm. The latter is the kind of premise needed by inverse flattening methods.

Here is an explicit infinite countermodel even on powers of 3 with unit support. For integer N>=2 put

    S={3(i+2N i^2)+1 : 0<=i<N},
    m=the smallest power of 3 greater than 2 max S.

All support points are units, and m=Theta(N^3). Pair sums are unique up to order: equality reduces to i+j+2N(i^2+j^2)=u+v+2N(u^2+v^2); reduction modulo 2N fixes the sums, since 0<=i+j<2N, and then fixes the sums of squares, hence unordered pairs. There is no wraparound because m>2 max S. Thus every nonzero ordered difference is unique modulo m.

Let mu=nu be uniform on S. Their join is 1/N, compared with the ambient-uniform baseline 1/m. This exceeds the baseline by Theta(N^2), and saturates the Cauchy bound exactly. Nevertheless for c=mu*reflected(mu),

    ||c||_2^2=(2N^2-N)/N^4,
    ||mu||_2^2=1/N,
    ||c||_2 / ||mu||_2=sqrt(2/N-1/N^2).

This ratio decays as m^(-1/6), exponentially in log m. There is substantial flattening despite maximal zero-shift alignment. Therefore even exact Cauchy saturation plus a large excess over the ambient baseline cannot establish a subexponential norm-retention premise. This example does NOT claim to be generated by Collatz. It rejects the generic implication, not the possibility of a Collatz-specific theorem, nor every possible quantitative use of an inverse theorem.

Near-Cauchy equality alone gives only normalized-vector proximity: for u=P/||P||_2 and v=D/||D||_2, ||u-v||_2^2=2(1-<u,v>). It supplies no additional additive structure: in the example u=v on a Sidon support.

## The proposed signed-scale identity checked on actual data

For m=3^j and classes modulo 3^s, let

    H_s=sum_classes P(class) D(class)/(m/3^s).

Then H_0=nC/m, H_j=the exact join, and H_j=H_0+sum_s(H_s-H_(s-1)). This is the conditional-expectation decomposition in the prior report. It does not make H_s monotone. Do not confuse these exact projected inner products with the earlier monotone upper certificates.

Summing over all admissible suffix masses, exact rational evaluation gives:

| r,A,L | baseline | successive scale contributions | exact Q |
|---|---|---|---|
|10,15,256|647/81|85/81, 26/27, 1/3, -1/3|10|
|10,15,4096|424/3|-5/3, 1/3|140|

In the first case R=9 alone has strictly positive contributions 11/27,26/27,4/9,4/3 at EVERY available nontrivial scale. This finite witness rules out a blanket rule that each such mass split must contain a negative scale. It does not address weaker asymptotic frequency or aggregate inequalities. The two full examples already show either sign of total covariance, so there is no universal nonpositive-covariance shortcut.

## Verification and decision

check.py verifies the Sidon difference energy for N=4,8,16,32 and the exact rational decomposition for the two pre-existing Collatz cases; each full count is checked against unsplit endpoint enumeration. All checks pass; RESULT.json retains every mass contribution. No larger search panel was launched.

Do not apply an inverse-convolution theorem directly from a large join. A repaired route must first prove either (a) many sufficiently large translated correlations, with enough total squared mass to give the quantitative norm-retention hypothesis, or (b) direct control of the particular zero-translation alignment using the arithmetic origin of P and D. Route (a) additionally needs a mechanism propagating an actual match to distinct translations; no such mechanism has been established, and the Sidon countermodel shows it cannot be generic. Prefer (b) unless such propagation is derived. The previous allowable count-exponent loss remains the success criterion; neither this audit nor the finite signed decomposition improves that exponent.
