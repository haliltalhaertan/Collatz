# Fixed-start killed Feynman--Kac feasibility

Date: 2026-09-07  
Status: bounded exploratory analysis  
Scope: the actual admissible microcanonical endpoint arrays for the fixed-start
`G` and `H_0` families. No sealed run, depth extension, numerical experiment,
Git/Drive operation, or canonical-state edit was performed.

Nothing below proves the Collatz conjecture. It does not prove E6-N2, B4, a
nonzero profile, or a polynomial lower bound.

## 1. Exact fixed-start object and admissible endpoints

Put

```text
alpha = log_2(3),  beta = alpha-1,
rho = beta/alpha,
p(y) = (1-rho) rho^y,  y in Z_{>=0},
xi = y-beta,  q(x)=exp(2 pi i 2^x).
```

Let `X_0=a` and `X_t=a+sum_(i=1)^t xi_i`. For a barrier `h`, define

```text
tau_h = inf{t>=0 : X_t>h}.
```

The convention in the audited bridge formula multiplies the row phases at
times `0,...,m-1`. Since all endpoint families below have `b<0`, for `h=0`
there is no distinction between also requiring the endpoint at time `m` to be
below the barrier.

The two fixed-start families are:

```text
H_0: m=r-4, a_H=-5 alpha,
     b_r=-13-beta-theta_r,
     b_r-a_H+beta*m=n_r=floor(beta*r)-8;

G:   m=r,   a_G=-4-alpha,
     b_r=-12-alpha-theta_r=-13-beta-theta_r,
     b_r-a_G+beta*m=n_r.
```

Thus the conditioning event is legitimate exactly when the displayed integer
`n_r` is nonnegative. This occurs for `r>=14`; `r=14,15` are the degenerate
`n_r=0` cases, while `r>=16` is the first nondegenerate range. A statement
uniform in `theta` must mean uniform over this admissible triangular array,
not conditioning on arbitrary continuum endpoints.

For either family let

```text
p_m(a,b) = P_a(X_m=b),
p_m^h(a,b) = P_a(X_m=b, tau_h>m),
L_m^h(a,b) = E_a[ product_(t=0)^(m-1) q(X_t);
                   X_m=b, tau_h>m ].
```

The conditional positive and complex killed bridge quantities are exactly

```text
Q_m^h(a,b)=p_m^h(a,b)/p_m(a,b),
K_m^h(a,b)=L_m^h(a,b)/p_m(a,b).
```

The free mass is explicit. If `k=b-a+beta*m` is a nonnegative integer, then

```text
p_m(a,b)=binom(k+m-1,m-1) (1-rho)^m rho^k.
```

Otherwise the endpoint is inadmissible and all three kernels above are zero.

## 2. Exact operator and renewal objects

On bounded functions on the reachable real states define

```text
(P_h f)(x) = 1_{x<=h} sum_(y>=0) p(y) f(x+y-beta),
(T_h f)(x) = 1_{x<=h} q(x) sum_(y>=0) p(y) f(x+y-beta).
```

Their lattice kernels satisfy

```text
P_h^m(a,b)=p_m^h(a,b),
T_h^m(a,b)=L_m^h(a,b).
```

Equivalently, with a formal marker `z` for the integer total,

```text
(T_h(z)f)(x)
 =1_{x<=h} q(x)(1-rho) sum_(y>=0)(rho*z)^y f(x+y-beta),
```

and the coefficient `[z^k] T_h(z)^m` selects the actual microcanonical
endpoint. This is the exact killed Feynman--Kac/operator object; no continuum
endpoint interpolation is needed.

The corresponding Green/resolvent kernels are

```text
G_h(s;a,b)=sum_(m>=0) s^m P_h^m(a,b),
F_h(s;a,b)=sum_(m>=0) s^m T_h^m(a,b).
```

For `P_h`, the strong Markov decomposition at ascending/descending ladder
epochs gives the usual half-line Wiener--Hopf/ladder-renewal representation.
It is this positive renewal object that supplies a killed local limit of order
`m^(-3/2)` between fixed points below the barrier. For `T_h`, multiplication by
the nonconstant `q(x)` destroys translation invariance, so scalar
Wiener--Hopf factorisation does not apply directly. One instead obtains an
infinite-dimensional boundary perturbation of the killed positive kernel.

The perturbation is genuinely short-range in depth. For every fixed `h`,

```text
sum_(ell>=0) sup_{h-ell-1 < x <= h-ell} |q(x)-1|
 <= 2*pi*2^h sum_(ell>=0)2^(-ell) < infinity.       (SR)
```

This follows from `|exp(iu)-1|<=|u|`. Hence a boundary-layer
renewal/Fredholm treatment is analytically plausible for a coefficient
asymptotic. It is not needed for the upper bound in Section 4.

## 3. Which reductions are and are not available

### Finite-state reduction: no exact reduction

The reachable group is generated by `1` and `beta`. Because `beta` is
irrational, it is dense modulo one. Any finite quotient compatible with both
translations and on which the row multiplier descends would force the
continuous function `q(x)=exp(2*pi*i*2^x)` to identify a dense family of
distinct translates. Since `q` is nonconstant and nonperiodic, this is
impossible. Thus there is no exact finite-state stationary reduction that
preserves the Feynman--Kac multiplier. Truncations may be numerical devices,
but they are not exact closures.

### Scalar spectral/Wiener--Hopf reduction: positive kernel only

For the translation-invariant free increment kernel, the characteristic
function

```text
phi(t)=E exp(i*t*(Y-beta))
       =(1-rho) exp(-i*beta*t)/(1-rho exp(i*t))
```

has mean zero, nonzero variance, span one in the time-dependent lattice coset,
and exponential moments near the origin. Classical ladder renewal therefore
applies to `P_h`. The complex kernel `T_h` is not a scalar convolution and
cannot inherit this factorisation without an operator-valued perturbation
argument and a zero-energy nonresonance check.

### Ladder-renewal reduction: feasible for the bounded killed term

The actual starts `a_G` and `a_H` are fixed negative numbers and `b_r` ranges
in the compact interval `(-14-beta,-13-beta]`. Therefore the standard killed
local-limit upper bound, if stated uniformly on admissible lattice endpoints,
has precisely the required form

```text
p_m^0(a,b_r) <= C_1*m^(-3/2),
p_m(a,b_r)   >= c_1*m^(-1/2).                         (BLL)
```

All distributional hypotheses are satisfied by the centred geometric law.
The remaining care is only to cite or prove `(BLL)` in a version allowing the
admissible time-dependent lattice cosets. It is a positive ballot/local-limit
statement, not an arithmetic-cancellation theorem.

## 4. Bounded preliminary lemma

**Lemma (exact domination).** For every admissible endpoint and every fixed
barrier,

```text
|K_m^h(a,b)| <= Q_m^h(a,b).
```

**Proof.** Every Feynman--Kac factor has modulus one. Apply the triangle
inequality to the unnormalised expectation defining `L_m^h`, obtaining
`|L_m^h(a,b)|<=p_m^h(a,b)`, and divide by the positive free endpoint mass.

**Corollary (fixed-start killed upper bound).** Under the standard positive
killed bridge estimate `(BLL)`, uniformly for the actual `G` and `H_0`
endpoint arrays,

```text
|K_m^0(a,b_r)| <= (C_1/c_1)/m.                         (KUB)
```

This is the bounded preliminary lemma sought here. Its main point is that a
complex killed-kernel coefficient asymptotic is unnecessary for an upper
bound: ordinary positive ladder renewal plus modulus domination already
controls the killed contribution at the conjectured order. A complex KFB
theorem remains relevant only for identifying a phase coefficient or proving
nontriviality.

## 5. Exactly one first missing estimate

After `(KUB)`, the first missing estimate for the fixed-start barrier route is
the **crossing-term bound**

```text
sup over actual admissible theta_r of
    | E_a[ product_(t=0)^(m-1) q(X_t);
           tau_0<=m | X_m=b_r ] | <= C/m.              (XUB)
```

Here `a` is either `a_G` or `a_H`. This is exactly the complement `R_m^0` in
the audited decomposition. Proving `(XUB)` together with `(KUB)` would give
the desired fixed-start `O(1/m)` upper bound. Proving `o(1/m)` instead would
also isolate the killed term as the leading profile, but that stronger claim
is not needed for the upper bound.

`(XUB)` is arithmetic: positive ballot probabilities cannot prove it, because
the crossing event has probability tending to one. It requires cancellation
of the dyadic/ternary phase on crossing paths, equivalently a joint
microcanonical Fourier/local-limit estimate. No finite-state, scalar
Wiener--Hopf, or ordinary ladder-renewal reduction found here supplies it.

## 6. Feasibility verdict

The fixed-start killed half of the proposed barrier mechanism is feasible and,
for upper bounds, substantially easier than the earlier KFB formulation made
it appear. It reduces to a standard positive killed local-limit theorem by the
exact modulus domination above. Infinite-state ladder renewal is the correct
probabilistic framework; an exact finite-state closure is impossible, and a
complex spectral/Fredholm treatment is necessary only if one asks for a
coefficient rather than a bound.

No load-bearing route-changing theorem or countertheorem was obtained, so no
audit stop rule fires. The only next mathematical target named by this review
is `(XUB)`.

Nothing in this document proves the Collatz conjecture.
