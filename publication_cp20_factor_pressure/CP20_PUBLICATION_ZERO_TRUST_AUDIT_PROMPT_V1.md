# 8. Mandatory stop rule

Stop the audit and report immediately if you establish any of the following:

1. a proposed principal theorem is stronger than its frozen source;
2. a close prior source actually subsumes a principal theorem;
3. a proof-critical constant or inequality has been mistranscribed;
4. the publication package depends on an unfrozen result;
5. chronology invalidates a claimed priority distinction.

Do not repair such a failure with new mathematics.
